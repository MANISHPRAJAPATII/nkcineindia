About Us

We don’t just click photos, we craft memories

NK Cine India is a creative photography and film production house. Whether it is the emotion of a wedding, the romance of a pre-wedding shoot or the excitement of taking delivery of a new car — we capture every moment with a cinematic eye.

Our team handles everything in-house: shooting, colour grading, editing and final delivery, so your story stays true from the first frame to the last.

  • Cinematic look & colour grading
  • Candid + traditional coverage
  • Photos, films, reels & teasers
  • Complete editing & production
  • Weddings, events & brand shoots
  • On-time delivery, every time
