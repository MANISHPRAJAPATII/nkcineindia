Apne MP4 reels yahan rakhein:

cine1.mp4
cine2.mp4
cine3.mp4
...

Website in numbered reels ko automatically Films section mein dikhayegi (cine1.mp4 se numbering shuru karein, beech mein number skip na karein; 100 tak supported). Apna pehle se configured YouTube ya MP4 video bhi CONFIG -> videos mein rakh sakte hain. Bade videos ke liye YouTube link behtar hai.
